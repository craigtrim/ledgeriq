## Task
Classify the given document text into **exactly one** of the following categories: `receipt`, `invoice`, `eob`, or `unknown`.

## Input
- document_text: {document_text}

## Categories

### receipt
Store or restaurant receipts showing consumer purchases.
- Merchant/store name
- Itemized products with prices
- Subtotal, tax, total
- Payment method
- Transaction date/time
- Store location

### invoice
Business-to-business invoices for goods or services.
- Invoice number (e.g., "Invoice #12345", "INV-2024-001")
- Bill To / Ship To addresses
- Line items with quantities and unit prices
- Payment terms (e.g., "Net 30", "Due upon receipt")
- Due date
- Vendor information

### eob
Explanation of Benefits from health insurance.
- Insurance company name (e.g., "Blue Cross", "Aetna", "UnitedHealthcare")
- Patient name and member/policy ID
- Healthcare provider name
- Service dates
- Procedure/CPT codes
- Amount billed, allowed, paid
- Patient responsibility (deductible, copay, coinsurance)

### unknown
Document does not clearly match the above categories.

## Rules
1. Match explicit type indicators first (e.g., "RECEIPT" header, "INVOICE #", "EXPLANATION OF BENEFITS").
2. Receipts have consumer purchases; invoices have business transactions; EOBs have medical/insurance details.
3. Do **not** classify receipts as: quotes, estimates, or pro forma invoices.
4. Do **not** classify invoices as: purchase orders, delivery notes, or packing slips.
5. Do **not** classify EOBs as: medical bills from providers (only insurance company EOBs).
6. If ambiguous or unclear, return `unknown`.

## Output
Return **exactly one word** with no additional text, punctuation, or formatting:
- `receipt`
- `invoice`
- `eob`
- `unknown`

{document_text}
